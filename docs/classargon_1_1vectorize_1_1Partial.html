Detailed Description

template<typename ScalarType, size_t Stride = 1>
class argon::vectorize::Partial< ScalarType, Stride >

One vector (or, for interleaved views, one group of Stride vectors) of a range, and how many of its leading lanes lie inside the range.

Template Parameters
ScalarTypeThe element type of the range.
StrideThe interleave stride: 1 for a plain view, 2 to 4 for an interleaved one, whose lanes are frames of Stride consecutive elements.

Every vector of a with_tail() view is full except possibly the last. Lanes past the end of the range are zero when loaded and are never written when stored; use active() to keep them out of reductions such as minimum or maximum, where a zero would matter.
